⚖ Friendship Court — UI Mockups

Mobile · dramatic courtroom · tap through the trial lifecycle

1 · Court in session

Prosecution vs Defense as stickmen with profile-pic heads, judge on the bench, live timer, the live opinion bar, and SWITCH SIDE. Reveal-on-send: spectators see only suspense — "Aakash is sealing their statement 📜… drops all at once" — never the live typing.

2 · Your turn (compose)

Full cinematic takeover: spotlight + pulsing "IT'S YOUR TURN". You draft privately ("🔒 hidden until you send"), then hit SLAM ⚡ reveal to drop it on the whole court at once. Evidence / @witness / rest-case actions + speaking-window ring.

3 · The vote shift

Between-round suspense beat: before/after bars animate the swing, avatars flip sides, big GUILTY / NOT GUILTY tap targets, a closing countdown, and the judge watching the swing.

4 · Verdict → jail → archive

Judge verdict, public override result, sentence, the animated jail with roses/notes/paper balls and a 6h countdown, observer-mode note, visitor actions, and the archive card for Highlights/Memories.
